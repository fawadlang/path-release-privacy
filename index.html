Arrow Release Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Arrow Release is published by Muhammad Fawad Ur Rehman under the Lang Systems banner. This policy explains how the Android and iOS game handles information. For questions or privacy requests, contact fawadlang@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your selected level, progress, results, hints, scores, and settings on your device so that you can continue playing. It keeps a local backup of your game profile to help recover progress. Arrow Release does not currently provide a game account or cloud synchronization. You can remove local game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Optional analytics

Firebase Analytics is off by default. If you turn it on in Me → Privacy preferences, the game sends gameplay and app usage events, such as level starts and completions, failed attempts, restarts, and hint use, together with level identifiers and related event values. Google may also process technical information associated with Analytics, such as app-instance and device information. You can turn Analytics off again in the same screen; the game then stops sending gameplay events and resets Analytics data on that device.

Optional crash reports (Android)

Firebase Crashlytics reporting is off by default and has a separate Crash reports control in Me → Privacy preferences. If you enable it, Crashlytics sends diagnostic information such as exception messages and stack traces, app and operating system versions, device characteristics, crash timestamps, and installation identifiers to help us diagnose failures. We add only app version, build type, and Unity runtime version as custom diagnostic fields; we do not attach your game profile or a custom player identifier. If you also enable Analytics, Crashlytics may include Analytics events as diagnostic breadcrumbs.

Crashlytics can keep crash information locally while reporting is off. Enabling reporting also sends those saved reports. If you disable reporting after enabling it, restart the app for the SDK collection change to take effect. This control is independent of Analytics and advertising choices. It does not delete reports already sent to Firebase.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled in versions that have not completed ad privacy setup. When enabled, Arrow Release uses Unity LevelPlay and ironSource Ads to request and display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. A rewarded ad grants one life only after the ad provider confirms the reward. The advertising SDK may process device or other identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, advertising data, and app performance data. These may be used to deliver and measure ads and for fraud prevention and security. Ad providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

In versions with advertising, Google UMP presents privacy choices where required before ad requests begin. Declining or withdrawing ad consent leaves the game playable, but ad-funded features may be unavailable. Where the consent message requires it, the game provides a way to revisit ad privacy options from Me → Privacy preferences. On iOS, any request for permission to track across apps will use Apple's system prompt if that feature is introduced.

Service providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase for optional Analytics and Android crash reports, and Unity LevelPlay and ironSource Ads for advertising when enabled. These providers may process information on our behalf or as described in their own notices. We do not sell your local game progress to other players or advertisers.

Your choices and requests

You can control Analytics and Android crash reporting separately in the app and, in versions with advertising, revisit ad privacy choices when the consent message requires it. You can also email fawadlang@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that apply where you live. Because game progress is stored locally, we may be unable to retrieve it from your device; clearing app data or uninstalling the game removes that local copy.

Children

Arrow Release is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us at fawadlang@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be available from the game's Privacy Policy link.
